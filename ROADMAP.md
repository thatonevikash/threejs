# Our Roadmap

We won't jump into React Three Fiber immediately. First, we'll build a solid understanding of **Three.js** and the fundamentals behind it.

## Phase 1 — 3D Fundamentals

We'll answer questions and explore concepts such as:

- What is Three.js?
- What is WebGL?
- GPU vs. CPU
- Coordinate systems
- Scenes
- Cameras
- Renderers
- Meshes
- Geometry
- Materials
- The animation loop

---

## Phase 2 — Objects

- Cube
- Sphere
- Plane
- Cylinder
- Torus
- Groups
- Helpers
- Axes

---

## Phase 3 — Cameras

- Perspective cameras
- Orthographic cameras
- Field of view (FOV)
- Near and far clipping planes
- OrbitControls

---

## Phase 4 — Lights

- Ambient lights
- Directional lights
- Hemisphere lights
- Spotlights
- Point lights
- Shadows

---

## Phase 5 — Materials

- Basic
- Standard
- Physical
- Phong
- Normal
- Toon

You'll learn why the same cube can look completely different depending on its material.

---

## Phase 6 — Textures

- UV mapping
- Image textures
- Normal maps
- Roughness maps
- Metalness maps
- HDRI

---

## Phase 7 — Models

- glTF
- GLB
- Blender workflow
- Compression
- Optimization

---

## Phase 8 — Animations

- Clock
- Delta time
- Animation mixers
- Keyframes
- GSAP

---

## Phase 9 — Shaders

One of the most exciting topics in the roadmap.

We'll cover:

- GLSL
- Vertex shaders
- Fragment shaders
- Noise
- Wave effects
- Dissolve effects
- Fire
- Water

---

## Phase 10 — Performance

This is where many tutorials stop, but performance is crucial for production-ready 3D experiences.

We'll learn about:

- Draw calls
- Instancing
- Frustum culling
- Level of detail (LOD)
- Texture optimization
- Geometry optimization

---

## Phase 11 — React Three Fiber

Only after understanding the engine will we move on to React Three Fiber.

Then we'll learn:

- Canvas
- `useFrame`
- `useLoader`
- Suspense
- Drei
- Performance patterns

---

## Phase 12 — Production Projects

We'll build:

- An Apple-style product viewer
- A portfolio hero section
- 3D cards
- Scroll-based storytelling
- A product configurator
- A particle background
- A solar system
- An Earth visualization
- An interactive dashboard
